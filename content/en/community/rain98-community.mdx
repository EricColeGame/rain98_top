## What the Rain98 Community Is Talking About Right Now

Rain98 is a narrative game set in Tokyo in 1998, built around an endless rain and a girl who wants everything to end. Two lonely teenagers, a city that never dries out, and a story that claims your choices will change the world — that is the pitch, and it is exactly the combination that keeps discussion threads alive. The demo puts that atmosphere in front of players for free, which is why the Rain98 community grew so quickly after the October 2026 release.

Here is where the conversation actually happens:

The demo shipped in nine languages, and the discussion board reflects that: you will find French players hunting a specific achievement, Spanish-language reviews praising the art and sound design, and English threads arguing about story direction — often on the same day.

## How to Join the Rain98 Community: Discord, Steam, and Social

Joining the Rain98 community takes about five minutes, and doing it in the right order saves you a lot of guesswork later.

| Step | Action | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Follow the game on Steam | Demo updates and patch notes appear in your feed automatically |
| 2 | Join the C#4R4CT3R Discord server | Watch parties, quick answers, and dev chat in real time |

A few etiquette notes go a long way. Search before posting a bug — the Daifugo objective question has been asked more than once. Keep spoilers for the second half of Chapter 2 behind a warning. And if you are reporting a problem, say what platform you are on, what you did right before it happened, and whether you can reproduce it. Reports written that way get answered.

The developers also run community moments around big events. When Rain98 was selected for The MIX Fall Game Showcase 2026, the team organized a watch party on the official Discord so fans could react to the new trailer together — a good example of how the studio treats its audience as a group rather than a marketing list.

### Tips for your first three hours

- **Explore before you optimize.** The demo adds new city areas, and several interactions only trigger if you wander instead of rushing the main text.
- **Play the minigames more than once.** Card games and side activities appear to feed into objectives, so a single win may not be enough.
- **Read in your first language.** With nine localization options, nothing is lost in translation — and the writing is the point.
- **Take notes on choices.** The game promises that decisions change the world, and theory-crafters will want receipts.
- **Save before experiments.** Branching scenes are easier to compare when you keep a manual save slot open.

The presentation is a recurring compliment in player experience threads. A Spanish-language review described the visuals, sound, character design, and environment work as genuinely beautiful, and singled out the 1998 setting as well realized. That tracks with the trailer footage, which leans hard on rain-soaked streets and detailed stills.

If you hit something similar, the most useful thing you can do is add to an existing thread rather than starting a new one. Include your save state, the exact objective name, and whether the counter moved at all. For achievements, check whether the unlock is tied to a specific scene or a repeatable minigame before assuming it is broken — that is the most common source of confusion in the Rain98 community.

## Sharing Content With the Rain98 Community: Streaming and Video Rules

Good news for creators: the studio's policy is unusually friendly.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Can I stream the demo? | Yes |
| Can I monetize that stream or video? | Yes, monetized or not |
| What must I include? | A link to the Rain98 Steam store page in the stream or video description |
| Is there an official hashtag? | Yes — #Rain98 |
| Are recorded gameplay videos allowed? | Yes |

The team has also made it easy to build a video around the game. There is a demo release trailer and a separate opening movie, plus a vocal version of the featured song performed by Rio Tsuchiya, the voice actress behind protagonist Reina Amehara. Reaction videos, soundtrack breakdowns, and "first three hours" blind playthroughs all have ready-made material to work with.

A few content ideas that tend to land well in the Rain98 community:

- **Blind playthroughs of the demo** with visible choices and reactions
- **Minigame guides**, especially for Daifugo, where players are already stuck
- **Localization comparisons** across two of the nine supported languages
- **Atmosphere essays** on how the game renders 1998 Tokyo
- **Theory videos** built only on what the demo actually shows

Just mark spoilers in thumbnails and opening seconds. The demo covers the first half of Chapter 2, and plenty of people are still working through it.

## Final Thoughts

What makes this scene worth following is that it is still small enough to influence. The developers read the Steam Community, run watch parties on Discord, and ask directly for demo feedback. Show up with specifics, be generous with spoiler tags, and the Rain98 community will return the favor.
