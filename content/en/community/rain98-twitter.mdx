## Why Rain98 Twitter Matters for New Players

Rain98 does not shout. There is no sprawling newsroom behind it — just a focused feed run by the development team, C#4R4CT3R, that posts when something real happens. That makes the account unusually high-signal: nearly every post carries information a player or creator can act on.

For a game built around a free demo, that matters more than it sounds. The demo is where most people will meet Reina for the first time, and the rules around sharing it — monetization, required links, recommended tags — are published there rather than buried in a press kit. If you skip the feed, you are essentially guessing.

| Post type | What it typically contains | Why it matters to you |
|---|---|---|
| Demo announcements | Steam link, playtime length, device support | Confirms where and when you can play free |
| Streaming guidelines | Monetization policy, required link, hashtag | Tells creators what is allowed before recording |
| Giveaway campaigns | Prizes, entry steps, eligibility notes | A chance at signed goods and promo items |
| Cast and character posts | Comments from the voice cast | Context on Reina and the game's tone |
| Reposts and quotes | Player screenshots, streamer clips | Shows what the community is actually doing |

Community reports suggest the account is deliberately small and personal rather than a marketing machine. At the time of this snapshot it showed roughly 164 posts — few enough that a new player can scroll the entire feed in one sitting and still catch up on everything.

### How to read the feed efficiently

- **Start with the pinned post.** It carries the current streaming guidelines and is the most consequential thing on the account.
- **Use the media tab.** Trailers, short clips, and cast comments live there and are faster to scan than the timeline.
- **Read quote posts, not just originals.** Giveaway entries and player reactions appear as quote reposts, which is often where the useful community chatter is.
- **Turn on notifications if you enter a campaign.** Deadlines are short and easy to miss.

## Rain98 Twitter Hashtags and Community Language

Hashtags do most of the organizing work in this community. Two matter, and they are not interchangeable: one is for general posting, the other is tied specifically to demo campaigns and entries.

| Hashtag | Where it is used | What to post with it |
|---|---|---|
| `#Rain98` | General posts, streams, screenshots, impressions | Clips, fan art, first-impression threads |
| `#Rain98Demo` | Demo-specific campaigns and giveaway entries | Quote reposts of the campaign post |
| Steam store link | Required in video descriptions for creators | Not a hashtag, but mandatory |

A practical habit: search `#Rain98` before you post. You will see how other players frame their clips, which scenes get shared most, and whether a campaign is currently running. Searching is also the fastest way to check whether a giveaway has already closed — quote reposts pile up quickly, and older campaign posts keep circulating long after entry windows end.

If you are posting about your own playthrough, `#Rain98` is the safer general tag. Reserve `#Rain98Demo` for demo-focused posts and official entry mechanics, so the tag stays useful for the team scanning entries.

## Rain98 Twitter Streaming Guidelines for Creators

For anyone planning to record or stream, the pinned guidelines are the single most useful post on the account. The short version: the demo is open to creators, and the team has made that permission explicit rather than case-by-case.

| Guideline | What it means in practice |
|---|---|
| Monetization | Allowed with or without monetization — no permission request needed |
| Content types | Both live streaming and uploaded video are covered |
| Required link | Include the Steam store page URL in the video description |
| Recommended tag | Use `#Rain98` when posting the stream or video |
| Scope | Applies to the demo build |

That is a friendlier policy than many small releases offer, and it removes the most common friction point: creators wondering whether they need to email anyone first. They do not.

Two practical tips follow from this. First, paste the store link into your description template before you go live, not after — it is easy to forget once a stream ends. Second, keep a screenshot of the guidelines post. Pinned posts get replaced, and having a dated record saves confusion if the rules are later revised.

When you do publish, point viewers toward [Rain98's Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3738760/Rain98/) so they can try the free demo themselves — the demo reportedly offers more than three hours of gameplay, which is enough for a full first-look video without running dry.

## Giveaways and Demo Campaigns on Rain98 Twitter

The demo launch was accompanied by a celebration giveaway, and its structure is a useful template for how these campaigns tend to work on the account.

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Prize one | Goods signed by voice actress Rio Tsuchiya — 4 winners |
| Prize two | Not-for-sale cassette tape — 5 winners |
| Prize selection | Winners cannot choose which prize they receive |
| Entry step one | Follow @csharp4r4ct3r |
| Entry step two | Quote repost the campaign post with `#Rain98Demo` |

A few things are easy to get wrong. The entry requires a **quote repost**, not a plain repost or a reply — a detail people skim past. The hashtag must be attached to that quote post. And because prizes are assigned rather than chosen, entering means accepting either outcome.

Community reports also note that the campaign post itself is the authoritative source for deadlines; third-party roundups often copy the prize list but drop the closing date. Always check the original.

### Tips for getting more out of the Rain98 Twitter community

- **Follow before a campaign, not during one.** Entry rules usually require you to already be following, and follow-then-unfollow patterns are easy to spot.
- **Quote repost with a real comment.** A short line about what interests you reads better than a bare hashtag and fits the spirit of the entry rules.
- **Check the cast posts.** Comments from the voice cast are where you learn about Reina's characterization — useful context for a review or first-impression video.
- **Watch for device notes.** Posts about playing on handheld gaming PCs are practical for viewers deciding whether the game suits their setup.
- **Do not spam the tag.** Repeating the same post clutters the feed and buries actual entries.
