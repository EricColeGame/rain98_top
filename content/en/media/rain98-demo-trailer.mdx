Gameplay alternates between two modes. Novel segments push the story forward and let you spend time with Reina; adventure segments let you leave the apartment and explore 1998 Shibuya. Doing something special for her earns a sticker for her Angel Passport, and each sticker advances the ritual to end the world — a neat mechanical hook that ties affection to apocalypse.

If you're weighing whether the Rain98 demo trailer oversells the interactivity, the honest answer is that the game is more reading than platforming. The Point & Click and 2D Platformer tags are real, but they describe seasoning, not the meal.

Player experience so far clusters around two reactions. Supporters praise the atmosphere, the art direction, and Reina's voice performance, calling the rain-and-Lo-Fi combination genuinely transporting. Critics tend to point at pacing and at how much of the runtime is spent reading rather than interacting — a fair complaint for anyone who arrived expecting the adventure segments to dominate.

Two things are worth remembering before you read too much into a 61% score. First, demo reviews on Steam skew harsher than full-game reviews because a free download costs nothing to rate. Second, the demo is explicitly incomplete: the studio has committed to expanding it through the end of chapter 2. A snapshot from launch week is not a verdict on a 10-to-15-hour game.

## FAQ

**Does the Rain98 demo trailer announce a full release date?**
No. It confirms the free demo's availability and reinforces the 2026 window for the full game, but no specific launch day has been given. The full version is currently listed as wishlist-only.

**How long is the demo, and will it get longer?**
The playable content runs from the story's opening through the first half of chapter 2, and the studio has described it publicly as a multi-hour slice. An update is planned to extend it through the end of chapter 2, with save continuity so you can pick up where you left off instead of replaying.

**Do I need to play the demo to understand the full game?**
Not necessarily, but the demo opens at the actual beginning of the story rather than dropping you into a later chapter, so it doubles as a genuine prologue. That also makes the Rain98 demo trailer a fairly accurate preview of the game's opening hours rather than a highlight reel.

**Where can I watch the Rain98 demo trailer?**
It's embedded on the demo's Steam store page, and the studio also promoted it through its official YouTube and Bilibili channels during The MIX Fall Game Showcase 2026. The Steam version is the most convenient, since the trailer, screenshots, and download link sit together.

**Is the demo a good indicator of the final game's quality?**
Treat it as a tone sample, not a review. The mixed early reception reflects an unfinished build and a slow-burn structure, and the planned chapter 2 expansion will change how complete the slice feels. If the Rain98 demo trailer's atmosphere hooked you, the demo is worth the download — just go in expecting a reading-heavy, deliberately paced experience.
