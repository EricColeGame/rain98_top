## What Kind of Game Is Rain98?

Rain98 is a Lo-Fi psychological suspense game about sharing a cramped, rain-drenched everyday life with a girl who wants the world to stop. You play through a story set partly in present-day Tokyo and partly in a nostalgic, neon-washed 1998 Tokyo, alongside Reina Amehara (voiced by Rio Tsuchiya), a young woman who introduces herself as "Angolmois."

The premise is deceptively cozy. You talk with Reina, run errands she asks of you, and wander a retro Shibuya together — all while quietly advancing a ritual meant to end the world. The store page frames it as two lonely youths whose choices change everything, which lines up with the "Multiple Endings" and "Choices Matter" tags.

| Gameplay Pillar | How It Plays |
| --- | --- |
| Novel segments | Story-driven scenes with Reina inside the apartment; dialogue and relationship building |
| Angel Passport stickers | Doing something special for Reina earns a sticker that pushes the end-of-world ritual forward |
| Shibuya exploration | Leave the apartment and walk a rainy, retro 1998 Tokyo district with Reina |
| Lo-Fi atmosphere | Rain audio, blurry blue lighting, and mellow music layered over creeping dread |
| Branching outcomes | Multiple endings driven by player choices |

That combination — comfort and quiet menace in the same frame — is the core hook. If you liked the slow-burn unease of cult favorite visual novels, the tag list ("Psychological Horror," "Anime," "Romance," "Mystery," "1990's") tells you exactly which audience this is aimed at.
