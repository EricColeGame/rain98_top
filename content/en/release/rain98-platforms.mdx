## Will Rain98 Come to PS5, Xbox, or Switch?

There is no announcement, no teaser, and no rating board leak to point at. Anyone telling you a Switch 2 version is "confirmed" is guessing.

A few honest guidelines for reading the situation:

- **Controller support is not a console port.** The Steam page lists Xbox controller support because the game accepts gamepad input on PC. That says nothing about an Xbox release.
- **User tags are noise, not data.** Rain98's tag list includes "2D Platformer," which is a community tag applied loosely — the game is a narrative adventure with point-and-click and visual novel elements, not a platformer. Do not build purchase expectations on tags.
- **Steam-first is the default path.** For self-published narrative games, console ports typically follow 6–18 months after a successful PC launch, if they happen at all.
- **Wishlists move the needle.** The most useful thing you can do if you want Rain98 on a console is to wishlist it on Steam and tell the developer directly through official channels.

The realistic forecast: expect the PC version first, expect a Steam Deck verdict near launch, and treat every other Rain98 platforms question as open until an official announcement appears.
