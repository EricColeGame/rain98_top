### Practical Tips for Your First Playthrough

- **Treat mood as a survival stat, not a side quest.** The description warns that upsetting Reina leads to terrible things. Assume it is not bluffing.
- **Keep a cash buffer.** Running out of money in a game where a character's happiness depends on being fed is a classic spiral.
- **Play with headphones.** The soundtrack and environmental audio — ringing public phones, beeping arcades, neon-lit Shibuya rain — are doing heavy narrative work.
- **Explore in the rain.** The city traversal is where the '90s Tokyo fantasy actually lands, not in the apartment.
- **Log your first impressions of Reina.** Psycho-thrillers that blur reality and delusion reward players who remember what they thought before the story started twisting.

The minigames deserve a note. In a game this tightly focused, variety activities are pacing tools. They give you a break from the tension of a single room and a single conversation partner. That is a smart structural choice for a story that could otherwise become claustrophobic.
