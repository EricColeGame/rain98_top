## Novel Segments vs. Adventure Segments

Rain98 alternates between two modes, and knowing which one you're in changes how you should play. Novel segments are reading-heavy: dialogue, narration, and choices that shape your relationship with Reina. Adventure segments loosen the reins, letting you interact with her directly and explore the city.

| Segment Type | What You Do | Playthrough Tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Novel | Read the story, pick dialogue responses, follow Reina's arc | Slow down on choices — they build the shared history the ritual depends on |
| Adventure | Interact with Reina, handle her requests, explore rainy Shibuya | Exhaust optional interactions before advancing the plot |
| Exploration | Walk 1998 Shibuya with Reina, soak in retro detail | Treat it as a scavenger hunt for character moments, not filler |

The adventure half is where a lot of the charm lives. The store page describes leaving the apartment and heading into a retro-styled Shibuya steeped in turn-of-the-millennium atmosphere — blurry blue lights, lo-fi music layered over rain, and a cozy mood with something uncomfortable sitting underneath it.

That contrast is the whole design thesis. The comfort is real, but so is the shadow. Reina's secret quietly creeps over the everyday life you're building, and the game leans on psychological suspense rather than jump scares.

## A Step-by-Step Rain98 Demo Playthrough Route

If you want a clean, complete run without wasting time, follow this order. It front-loads the systems that matter and leaves room for a second pass.

## Is the Rain98 Demo Worth Playing First?

Player experience has been divided. At the time of writing, the demo's Steam reviews sit at "Mixed," with 61% of 233 reviews positive. That's a normal spread for a narrative demo — people who click with the slow, melancholy pacing tend to love it, and people expecting faster horror bounce off.

The mature content warning is worth reading before you start. The developers flag depictions of self-harm, suicide, drug use, religious rituals, psychological and physical violence, alcohol and tobacco use, and indirect sexual content. This is not a cozy game wearing a cute hat, even when it looks like one.

| Play It If You Like | Skip It If You Want |

## FAQ

**How long is a Rain98 demo playthrough?**
Most players finish the available content in roughly two to three hours, since the demo covers the story's opening through the first half of chapter 2. Reading speed and exploration time change that number a lot.

**Does demo progress carry over to the full game?**
The store page confirms the demo will be updated to include all of chapter 2, and you'll be able to resume from the point you reached before the update. Carryover into the full release hasn't been detailed, so keep your saves.

**Can I miss stickers during a Rain98 demo playthrough?**
Yes. Angel Passport stickers come from optional interactions and small favors for Reina, not just main story progress. Accept her requests and explore Shibuya thoroughly to fill the passport.

**Is the demo the same as the full game's opening?**
It's the genuine opening of the story, cut off partway through chapter 2. The full version expands well beyond it, with an estimated 10 to 15 hours of total gameplay and multiple endings driven by your choices.
